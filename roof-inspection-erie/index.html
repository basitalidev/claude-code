Roof Inspection Erie FAQ

How much does a roof inspection cost in Erie?

Pricing depends on the size, pitch and material of your roof and the type of inspection you need. Call or request an inspection online and we'll give you a clear, upfront price for your Erie property before we schedule — no hidden fees.

How quickly can you inspect my roof in Erie?

We typically schedule roof inspections in Erie within 24–48 hours, with priority scheduling for real estate deadlines and after major hailstorms. Your photo report usually arrives the same day or next business day.

Do newer Erie homes need roof inspections?

Yes. Even newer roofs can be damaged by hail and wind, and installation issues can shorten roof life. An inspection confirms your roof is performing as it should.

What should I do after a hailstorm in Erie?

Note the date of the storm, look for dents on gutters, vents and vehicles, and schedule a hail damage roof inspection while the evidence is fresh. If damage is confirmed, our report helps you file a well-documented insurance claim.
