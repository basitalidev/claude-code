Why Denver Property Owners Choose Our Commercial Roof Inspection

For property owners and managers, the roof is one of the largest capital assets on the balance sheet — and one of the most likely to cause disruption when it fails. Regular commercial roof inspections extend roof life, support warranty compliance and turn surprise emergencies into planned maintenance.

We inspect office buildings, retail centers, warehouses, churches, schools, HOA communities, apartment complexes and mixed-use properties throughout the Denver metro, with reporting suited for budgeting, capital planning and insurance.

HOA & Multi-Family Roof Inspections

HOA boards and property managers need consistent, comparable information across many buildings. We inspect communities building by building and deliver organized reports that make it easier to prioritize repairs, plan reserves and communicate with residents.

Flat Roofs in Colorado's Climate

Low-slope roofs face unique challenges in Denver: ponding water that freezes and thaws, UV degradation of membranes, hail punctures and wind uplift at perimeters. Twice-yearly inspections — spring and fall — are the standard recommendation for most commercial roofs.

How often should a commercial roof be inspected?

Most commercial roofs should be inspected twice a year, typically spring and fall, plus after any significant hail or wind event.

Can you identify wet insulation under a flat roof?

Yes. Infrared thermal imaging and moisture readings help locate trapped moisture beneath the membrane so only affected areas need replacement.

Do you provide reports for insurance or lenders?

Yes. Our commercial reports include photos, condition ratings and remaining-life estimates suitable for insurers, lenders and capital planning.
