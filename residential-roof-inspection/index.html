Why Denver Property Owners Choose Our Residential Roof Inspection

Your roof is the single most important system protecting your home, yet most homeowners only think about it when something goes wrong. A professional residential roof inspection gives you a clear, objective picture of your roof's condition before a small problem turns into a ceiling stain, a mold issue, or an unplanned replacement.

In the Denver metro, roofs age faster than the national average. Intense UV at high altitude dries out asphalt shingles, daily freeze-thaw swings work fasteners and sealants loose, Front Range wind events lift and crease shingles, and spring and summer hail can bruise a roof in minutes. A residential roof inspection catches this wear early, when repairs are still simple and inexpensive.

When to schedule a residential roof inspection

  • You've owned your home for several years and never had the roof inspected
  • Your roof is 12–15+ years old and you want to plan a budget
  • You see granules in the gutters, curling shingles or dark streaks
  • You notice stains on ceilings or in the attic
  • You're planning solar panels and want to confirm the roof will outlast them

What to Expect During Your Home Roof Inspection

Your inspector starts with a walk around the exterior, looking at gutters, downspouts, siding and other metal surfaces for clues about storm history and drainage. Next, where it's safe, they walk every slope of the roof, examining shingles, flashing and penetrations up close. Steep, tile or fragile roofs are documented by drone instead.

If the attic is accessible, the inspector checks for daylight, water staining, wet insulation, frost, and adequate intake and exhaust ventilation — the hidden causes of ice dams and premature shingle failure. Most homes take 45 to 90 minutes from start to finish.

Your Roof Inspection Report

You'll receive a written report with dozens of labeled photos, each finding rated by urgency: monitor, repair soon or needs attention now. The report includes an estimate of the roof's remaining service life so you can plan ahead instead of reacting to an emergency.

Because we focus on inspection rather than selling roofs, our recommendations are straightforward. If your roof is in good shape, the report says so.

FAQ

Residential Roof Inspection FAQ

How often should a Denver homeowner have their roof inspected?

At least once a year, ideally in late summer or early fall after hail season ends, and again after any significant hail or wind event. Roofs older than 15 years benefit from more frequent checks.

Can you tell how old my roof is?

We can usually estimate a roof's age from the shingle style, granule wear, sealant condition and other details. If you have permit records or installation paperwork, we'll use those to confirm.

Do you inspect the attic as part of a residential roof inspection?

Yes, when there is safe access. The attic often reveals leaks, ventilation problems and moisture damage that can't be seen from the roof surface.

Will I get a repair estimate?

Our report identifies what needs attention and why. You're free to share it with any roofer you choose to get repair pricing — many clients find the photo documentation makes quotes faster and more accurate.
