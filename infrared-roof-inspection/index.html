Infrared & Leak Detection Inspection

Why Denver Property Owners Choose Our Infrared & Leak Detection Inspection

A roof leak can travel along rafters, decking and insulation before it shows up on a ceiling, sometimes many feet from where it started. Repairing the wrong spot wastes money and leaves the real problem — and the hidden water damage — in place.

Our leak detection inspections combine experience with technology. Infrared cameras reveal temperature differences caused by wet materials, while moisture meters confirm what the camera suggests. The result is a precise diagnosis and a targeted repair recommendation.

Common Leak Sources on Denver Roofs

  • Pipe boots — rubber collars crack quickly under high-altitude UV.
  • Chimney and wall flashing — sealant fails and metal separates over freeze-thaw cycles.
  • Valleys — concentrated water and snowmelt expose installation shortcuts.
  • Skylights — curb seals and flashing kits age out before the roof does.
  • Ice dams — water backs up under shingles at cold eaves.
  • Attic condensation — poor ventilation can look like a roof leak.

Infrared for Flat and Commercial Roofs

On low-slope roofs, water that gets under the membrane spreads through insulation and can go undetected for years. Infrared scans, typically performed in the evening as the roof cools, map wet areas so repairs can target only damaged sections instead of replacing the entire roof.

Can infrared find every roof leak?

Infrared is very effective at locating wet materials, but conditions such as weather and roof type affect results. We always combine it with visual inspection and moisture readings.

Is it a roof leak or condensation?

Both can produce attic moisture and ceiling stains. Our inspection looks at ventilation, insulation and leak patterns to determine the actual cause.

Should I wait until it rains again?

No. Most leak sources can be found in dry weather, and waiting allows hidden damage to spread.
