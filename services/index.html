Choose Your Roof Inspection

Not sure which inspection you need? Call (303) 555-0142 and we'll point you in the right direction.

🏠

Residential Roof Inspection

A complete condition assessment of your home's roof, flashing, ventilation, gutters and attic, with an estimate of remaining roof life.
🧊

Hail Damage Roof Inspection

Test-square documentation, impact mapping and collateral evidence so you know whether you have a legitimate hail claim.
🌬️

Storm & Wind Damage Inspection

Checks for creased, lifted or missing shingles, displaced ridge caps and exposed fasteners after Front Range wind events.
🔑

Real Estate Roof Inspection

Buyer, seller and pre-listing roof inspections that go beyond a general home inspection — giving you leverage and no surprises at closing.
📜

Roof Certification

When a lender, insurer or buyer needs proof of roof condition, we inspect and, if the roof qualifies, issue a written certification letter.
📋

Insurance Claim Roof Inspection

Thorough, organized storm damage documentation to support your homeowners insurance claim, plus guidance for the adjuster's visit.
🏢

Commercial Roof Inspection

Flat and low-slope inspections — TPO, EPDM, modified bitumen, built-up and metal — for commercial, HOA and multi-family properties.
🛸

Drone Roof Inspection

High-resolution aerial imaging for steep, tall, tile or slate roofs where walking isn't safe or could cause damage.
🌡️

Infrared & Leak Detection Inspection

Thermal imaging and moisture readings that pinpoint trapped water and hidden leak paths in decking, insulation and flat roofs.
🗓️

Annual Roof Maintenance Inspection

A yearly check-up — ideally after hail season — that catches failing sealant, loose flashing and clogged drainage while fixes are inexpensive.
🏗️

New Roof & Warranty Inspection

Independent verification that a new roof was installed to manufacturer specifications and code, protecting your warranties.
🌀

Attic & Ventilation Inspection

Evaluates intake, exhaust, insulation and condensation — the hidden causes of ice dams, mold and premature shingle aging.
